# Private Charter Office — Zero-Cost Acquisition Plan

Paid acquisition stays OFF until supply-side referral terms are validated.

## Search-intent pages to build
Priority 1:
- French Riviera yacht charter — Saint-Tropez, Cannes, Monaco
- Greece private yacht charter — Cyclades, Ionian, Dodecanese
- Croatia private yacht charter — Split, Dubrovnik, Hvar
- Balearics yacht charter — Ibiza, Mallorca, Menorca
- Amalfi Coast yacht charter

Each page should lead to the same private brief rather than display invented inventory. Content should answer genuine planning questions: season, routing, typical trip length, guest considerations, broad cost drivers, embarkation points and what information is needed to source a yacht. Never publish invented live availability or partner inventory.

## Future upstream lead sources
Activate after at least one credible supply partner is available:
- Luxury villa agencies
- Private aviation advisers/brokers
- Five-star hotel concierge teams
- Independent luxury travel advisers
- Destination management companies
- Luxury wedding/event planners
- Private concierge companies
- Family-office / private-client service businesses where appropriate

Pitch: Private Charter Office qualifies and manages the yacht-sourcing request while an established charter professional contracts with the client. Any upstream referral share must come from available economics and be agreed in writing; never promise a percentage before the downstream commission is known.

## Measurement
Track by source:
- Visits / introductions
- Brief starts
- Qualified briefs
- Partner-routed briefs
- Bookings
- Gross booking value
- Commission expected
- Commission paid

Do not optimize for traffic alone. Primary validation metric is qualified charter mandates that can be monetized through written partner terms.
