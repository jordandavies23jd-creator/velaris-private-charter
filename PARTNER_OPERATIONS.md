# Private Charter Office — Partner Operations

## Validation gate
Do not incorporate, buy a domain, run paid ads, or represent Private Charter Office as an established brokerage until supply-side validation is achieved.

Target: 3–5 charter businesses agreeing in writing to accept qualified introductions and pay an agreed referral/commission on completed business.

## Partner qualification record
For every prospective partner capture:
- Company and primary contact
- Website and operating territories
- Charter type / typical minimum budget
- Relationship status: prospect / contacted / negotiating / active / paused / declined
- Referral percentage or fixed fee
- Commission calculation base
- Attribution trigger and attribution window
- Repeat-booking protection
- Cancellation treatment
- Payment trigger and payment timing
- Whether client remains attributed to Private Charter Office
- Whether co-branding / white-label is permitted
- Agreement date and evidence/location of written terms
- Notes and exclusions

## Required terms before activation
1. Partner accepts introduced charter clients.
2. Referral/commission amount or formula is written and unambiguous.
3. Attribution starts from a defined introduction (normally timestamped email/lead registration).
4. Attribution window is defined.
5. Calculation base is defined (charter fee, broker commission, platform fee, or other).
6. Payment trigger is defined (deposit, full payment, embarkation, completion, etc.).
7. Payment timing is defined.
8. Cancellation/refund treatment is defined.
9. Repeat-client / repeat-charter treatment is defined.
10. Contracting party and fund handling are clear: Private Charter Office does not hold charter funds in V1.

## Reply playbook
### Positive / interested
Thank them, confirm the sourcing model, and ask for their written referral/introducer terms covering commission, attribution, payment timing, repeat bookings and cancellations. Avoid claiming inventory, memberships or existing booking volume.

### Tell us more
Explain: one private client brief is qualified by destination, dates, party size, budget and requirements; suitable enquiries are introduced to selected established charter professionals; the selected professional controls availability, contract, payment and charter delivery; Private Charter Office seeks an agreed supplier-side referral fee on converted introductions.

### Application required
Record the application route. Do not submit forms requiring a registered company, regulated/trade credentials or paid membership until the business is validated and the owner approves formation/spend.

### Commission proposal
Normalize the offer into: effective % of charter value (where calculable), calculation base, attribution window, payment trigger, payment timing, repeat protection and cancellation rules. Do not accept ambiguous terms as a validated agreement.

### Call requested
Prepare questions and facts first. Owner handles calls requiring identity, commitments or negotiation authority.

### Rejection
Record reason. Do not argue. Use objections to refine future outreach.

## Client operating pipeline
New enquiry → Qualified → Routed → Offers received → Shortlisted → Introduced → Booked → Commission due → Commission paid → Closed.

### Qualification minimum
- Name and usable contact details
- Destination / cruising area
- Dates or flexibility
- Guest count
- Indicative budget
- Material yacht/preferences requirements
- Confirmation that the client understands the charter professional handles contract and funds

## V1 boundaries
- Yacht charter only; no package of yacht + villa + flight.
- No client charter funds held.
- No claim to own yachts or represent inventory not supplied by a partner.
- No fake partner logos, testimonials, memberships or availability.
- Do not collect passport, card, bank or other unnecessary sensitive information in the initial brief.
- Final charter contract, availability, KYC/payment requirements and delivery remain with the selected charter professional.
