# Private Charter Office — Partner Operations

## Validation gate
Do not incorporate, buy a domain, run paid ads, or represent Private Charter Office as an established brokerage until supply-side validation is achieved.

Target: 3–5 charter businesses agreeing in writing to accept qualified introductions and pay an agreed referral/commission on completed business.

## Partner qualification record
For every prospective partner capture:
- Company and primary contact
- Website and operating territories
- Charter type / typical minimum budget
- Relationship status: prospect / contacted / negotiating / active / paused / declined
- Referral percentage or fixed fee
- Commission calculation base
- Attribution trigger and attribution window
- Repeat-booking protection
- Cancellation treatment
- Payment trigger and payment timing
- Whether client remains attributed to Private Charter Office
- Whether co-branding / white-label is permitted
- Agreement date and evidence/location of written terms
- Notes and exclusions

## Required terms before activation
1. Partner accepts introduced charter clients.
2. Referral/commission amount or formula is written and unambiguous.
3. Attribution starts from a defined introduction (normally timestamped email/lead registration).
4. Attribution window is defined.
5. Calculation base is defined (charter fee, broker commission, platform fee, or other).
6. Payment trigger is defined (deposit, full payment, embarkation, completion, etc.).
7. Payment timing is defined.
8. Cancellation/refund treatment is defined.
9. Repeat-client / repeat-charter treatment is defined.
10. Contracting party and fund handling are clear: Private Charter Office does not hold charter funds in V1.

## Source attribution
Every customer mandate must have a source before it is routed to a charter partner.

Source record:
- Source/organisation name
- Source type: direct, upstream partner, EA/PA network, HNW network, wealth/family office, other
- Unique referral code
- Contact
- Status
- Attribution window
- Agreed share of PCO commission, if applicable
- Written-terms status and agreement date

Rules:
1. Partner-specific links use a referral code, e.g. `?ref=CVVILLAS` once that source is approved.
2. The referral code is captured with the mandate and resolved to the source record.
3. Direct enquiries are explicitly recorded as direct/website/search/referral rather than left unattributed.
4. Source attribution is frozen when the mandate is qualified unless a documented correction is required.
5. No upstream commission is promised or paid unless written terms exist.
6. Upstream share is calculated from commission actually received by PCO, not from charter value, unless a signed agreement expressly says otherwise.
7. The booking record must show gross PCO commission, introducer share and net PCO commission.

## Reply playbook
### Positive / interested
Thank them, confirm the sourcing model, and ask for their written referral/introducer terms covering commission, attribution, payment timing, repeat bookings and cancellations. Avoid claiming inventory, memberships or existing booking volume.

### Tell us more
Explain: one private client brief is qualified by destination, dates, party size, budget and requirements; suitable enquiries are introduced to selected established charter professionals; the selected professional controls availability, contract, payment and charter delivery; Private Charter Office seeks an agreed supplier-side referral fee on converted introductions.

### Application required
Record the application route. Do not submit forms requiring a registered company, regulated/trade credentials or paid membership until the business is validated and the owner approves formation/spend.

### Commission proposal
Normalize the offer into: effective % of charter value (where calculable), calculation base, attribution window, payment trigger, payment timing, repeat protection and cancellation rules. Do not accept ambiguous terms as a validated agreement.

### Call requested
Prepare questions and facts first. Owner handles calls requiring identity, commitments or negotiation authority.

### Rejection
Record reason. Do not argue. Use objections to refine future outreach.

## Client operating pipeline
New enquiry → Qualified → Routed → Offers received → Shortlisted → Introduced → Booked → Commission due → Commission paid → Closed.

### Qualification minimum
- Name and usable contact details
- Destination / cruising area
- Dates or flexibility
- Guest count
- Indicative budget
- Material yacht/preferences requirements
- Confirmation that the client understands the charter professional handles contract and funds

## First real mandate SOP
1. **Receive** — create a charter brief immediately; preserve original source/referral code.
2. **Qualify** — verify contact, destination, dates, guests, realistic budget and material requirements. Mark qualified only when usable by a broker.
3. **Select suppliers** — choose a small number of partners whose territory, budget and relationship terms fit. Do not spray the mandate to the full list.
4. **Register introduction** — create a route record before/when the mandate is sent so attribution is timestamped.
5. **Collect offers** — record yacht, charter fee, extras, confirmed availability, expiry and concise differentiators.
6. **Shortlist** — normally present about three genuinely suitable options; do not manufacture choice when fewer credible options exist.
7. **Introduce** — once the client chooses, connect the client/representative to the selected established charter professional. PCO does not take charter funds.
8. **Book** — record booking value, supplier, selected offer and supplier-side commission expected.
9. **Calculate source share** — if an approved upstream agreement applies, calculate its agreed percentage from PCO commission according to written terms.
10. **Reconcile money** — record when PCO commission becomes due, when received and when any introducer share is paid.
11. **Close** — retain source → mandate → supplier → offer → booking → commission traceability.

## Supplier call checklist
For any supplier willing to discuss introducer business, establish in writing after the call:
- What exactly counts as a qualifying introduction?
- How is the client registered/protected?
- What commission/referral amount does PCO receive and what is the calculation base?
- When is commission earned and when is it paid?
- What happens on cancellation/refund?
- Are repeat charters protected, and for how long?
- Does the supplier require exclusivity or minimum volume? (PCO should avoid these in V1.)
- What information should accompany a qualified mandate?
- Who owns the client relationship during sourcing and after booking?
- Can PCO use supplier-provided yacht material in a client shortlist?

Practical test question: **If PCO sent a qualified €150,000 charter client tomorrow, what information would you need, what would happen next, and exactly how would PCO be paid if the charter completed?**

## Commercial document checklist
Before live referrals, prepare two short written agreements for professional review:

### Downstream introducer terms
- Parties and role
- Qualifying introduction
- Client registration/attribution
- Commission formula and calculation base
- Payment trigger/timing
- Cancellation/refund treatment
- Repeat booking treatment
- No exclusivity/minimum volume unless explicitly agreed
- PCO does not hold charter funds or deliver the charter
- Confidentiality/data handling
- Termination and accrued commission protection

### Upstream referral terms
- Parties and source/referral code
- Qualifying referred mandate
- Existing-known-client exclusion
- Attribution window
- Share of commission actually received by PCO
- Payment timing after PCO receipt
- Cancellation/refund treatment
- Repeat booking treatment
- No client charge for the referral
- No authority for source to bind PCO or charter supplier
- Confidentiality/data handling
- Termination and accrued referral protection

## V1 boundaries
- Yacht charter only; no package of yacht + villa + flight.
- No client charter funds held.
- No claim to own yachts or represent inventory not supplied by a partner.
- No fake partner logos, testimonials, memberships or availability.
- Do not collect passport, card, bank or other unnecessary sensitive information in the initial brief.
- Final charter contract, availability, KYC/payment requirements and delivery remain with the selected charter professional.
