Before the first introduction.

Return to handover desk · Partnership tracker

Complete these checks with each chosen charter business and record the written evidence in the protected desk. This page contains guidance only; it does not save approvals or agree commercial terms.

  1. Business and recipient. Verify the contracting business, published contact route and named person responsible for the enquiry. Confirm that the exact handover email is authorised to receive customer details.
  2. Charter suitability. Confirm destinations, crewed charter coverage, guest limits and budget range. Confirm who supplies quotes and handles availability, the charter contract and customer funds.
  3. Written introduction terms. Agree the commission basis, exclusions, attribution of new and existing clients, evidence required and attribution period. Record when commission becomes payable and what happens after cancellation or refund. Do not assume a percentage.
  4. Response and continuity. Agree a receipt acknowledgement deadline, named owner, next-step timing and a backup contact. Agree how to decline an unsuitable brief and update the office if ownership changes.
  5. Customer permission. Confirm the customer's current requirements, year of travel, budget basis and explicit sharing permission. Ready customer interest and an approved partner are separate requirements.
  6. Record approval. Put dated written evidence or message references for the checks above in the partner approval field. Only then register the verified partner. A prospect marked agreed does not activate a charter business.

For customer referral partners

Confirm whether genuine yacht enquiries arise, the typical trip and budget, and how referrals can be made with the customer's permission. Use a written trial or performance-based agreement where appropriate. Any referral compensation must be agreed from Private Charter Office commission actually received. Do not promise upfront membership fees, blanket discounts or payments to employed assistants. Paid arrangements require Jordan's specific budget approval.

When the first customer is ready

Review the saved brief, qualify it as ready, select one approved partner and queue the introduction. Monitor the recorded send result, receipt acknowledgement, named acceptance and customer update. If a send outcome is uncertain, inspect Gmail Sent before any retry. If the partner declines or misses the agreed deadline, contact them and use the audited reassignment process before choosing a replacement.

Do not close the introduction until the customer update has been sent and direct contact has been confirmed. A saved enquiry or partner acceptance does not confirm a booking.
