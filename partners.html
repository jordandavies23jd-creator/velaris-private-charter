FOUNDING PARTNER PROPOSAL

25%

Our current proposed founding-partner share is 25% of the commission actually received by Private Charter Office on an attributable completed transaction.

There is no joining fee and no minimum referral volume. Attribution, client protection, payment timing, cancellations and repeat-business treatment are agreed before live referrals begin.

The 25% share is a proposed commercial term, subject to written agreement for each partner. It is not 25% of the charter fee.
